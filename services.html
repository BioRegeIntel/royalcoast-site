What We Build

Our Services

Residential, commercial and specialty construction — with plumbing, electrical, roofing and structural work handled in-house.

01 · Residential Construction

Homes built right, from the ground up.

We build and renovate homes across Texas — from ground-up custom builds and tiny homes to kitchens, baths and whole-house renovations. Because our crews cover plumbing, electrical, roofing and structural work in-house, you are not waiting on four different subs to show up before your project can move.

  • Custom Homes & Tiny Homes — Ground-up residential construction at any scale, from compact tiny homes to full custom builds — designed, permitted and delivered by one team.
  • Kitchen & Bathroom Remodels — The two rooms that decide how a house feels. Layout, cabinetry, tile, fixtures and lighting coordinated so the trades are not working against each other.
  • Additions & ADUs — Room additions, second stories and accessory dwelling units — engineered and permitted, then tied cleanly into the existing structure and roofline.
  • Full Home Renovations — Whole-house renovation with one point of contact and a schedule that accounts for material lead times, not just labor.
  • Roofing, Plumbing & Electrical — The systems most contractors farm out, we handle ourselves — which is why our schedules hold when other jobs stall.

02 · Commercial Construction

Commercial work that respects your opening date.

On a commercial job the schedule is the budget. Every week of delay is rent paid on a space earning nothing. We sequence commercial work around that reality — including night and weekend phases when the site demands it.

  • New Build-Outs, Interior & Exterior — White box to occupancy, coordinated with landlord requirements, building management and the city.
  • Retail, Office & Medical Spaces — Space types with their own code and inspection realities. Medical build-outs in particular are unforgiving — we plan for that up front.
  • Restaurants & Hospitality Builds — Kitchens, grease systems, ventilation, fire suppression and front-of-house finish, delivered as one coordinated scope.
  • Structural Expansions & Renovations — Expanding or reworking an existing building, including occupied-space phasing that keeps your operation running.
More on Commercial Construction
03 · Specialty Projects

The projects other builders turn down.

Some builds do not fit a standard template. Barndominiums, unusual sites, mixed-use structures, projects another contractor walked away from. That is where the phrase we have used for years actually gets tested: no job too tough.

  • Barndominiums — Stylish, functional spaces combining modern comfort with rustic charm — engineered properly, not just a shell with rooms dropped inside it.
  • Concept-to-Completion Builds — From design and permitting to the final nail, we handle every step. One contract, one team, one person accountable for the result.
  • Difficult & Rescued Projects — Jobs that stalled, sites with complications, or scopes that grew past the original builder. We are often the second call, and we are used to it.
